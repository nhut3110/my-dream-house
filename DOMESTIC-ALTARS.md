# Ground-floor domestic altar study

Dimensions and positions are in `src/domestic-altars.js`. This is a spatial proposal informed by common Vietnamese customs; household and regional ritual practices vary. No age-based auspicious bearing is claimed.

- Ông Địa–Thần Tài: 610 × 480 × 805 mm cabinet beside the front of the curved stair, facing the main entrance. It stays outside the stair footprint, with over 1.2 m of passage to its right. Sealed joinery backing; symbolic ceramic figures.
- TV stays at its original centre (z=3.7 m), with a 1.60 m panel and 1.50 m credenza so the shrine fits beside it. Welcome mat stays clear of the shrine.
- Ông Táo: 480 × 330 mm independent shelf, surface 2.02 m above floor; moved to the dry left end of the sink return, backing onto the tiled rear partition. It sits beside the sink bowl, above faucet height and clear of the cooking run.
- Small upper bedroom wardrobe: reduced from 1.90 m to 1.60 m and shifted inside the room boundary, removing the 200 mm protrusion toward the landing/common area.
- Ceramic figures and tablet are symbolic study geometry. Incense sticks are unlit; the model is not a fire-safety design. Noncombustible offering surfaces are represented. Real installation needs appropriate heat/clearance review, secure wall anchors and access for cleaning/offerings.

References consulted:
- https://jos.husc.edu.vn/UPLOADS/numbers/70/1062_fulltext_cs2_10._nguyen_van_quang.pdf — regional variation in domestic worship and placement of Táo Quân.
- https://thebox.com.vn/goi-y-ve-bo-tri-ban-tho-ong-cong-ong-tao-trong-bep/ — dedicated elevated kitchen shelf and protection from grease/heat.
- https://spacet.vn/blog/kien-thuc-noi-that/kich-thuoc-va-cach-dat-ban-tho-ong-tao-cuc-chuan-theo-phong-thuy — placement away from sink and directly over cooking heat.

New altar geometry is original procedural Three.js geometry; no external model or image is redistributed.
